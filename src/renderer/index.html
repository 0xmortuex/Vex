Send everything through Tor or a proxy
Applies to every tab and container at once, survives a restart, and can be checked: Vex asks what address the internet sees with the route and without it.
Sites that always go through a route
Name a site and it opens in a routed session of its own, every time — without routing the rest of your browsing.
Vex is not a VPN service and does not run one — this routes the browser through Tor or through a proxy you already have. Anything outside Vex goes the way it always did.
